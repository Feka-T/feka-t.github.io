Policy Ratification: Strategic Asset Allocation 2024

The following policy changes have been vetted through the internal risk hierarchy and are presented for final Board authorization.

Strategic Justification

"This policy update aligns with our 5-year strategic plan to diversify into high-yield domestic equities. The findings from our SUC_004 Trend Analysis indicate a sustainable growth window in the financial sector. Both the Department Head and CEO have endorsed the updated VaR limits as necessary for these returns."

Executive Summary of Changes

  • Equity Limit: Increased from 25% to 35% of total portfolio.
  • ESG Compliance: Added strict exclusion for high-carbon emitting industries per 2024 mandates.
  • Rebalancing: Frequency shifted from Quarterly to Monthly for tighter risk control.
